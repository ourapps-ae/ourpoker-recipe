# Texas Hold’em - agreed house rules

Finalised 18 October 2026 from the six rounds of choices. This is a single-table no-limit elimination tournament with the specific session and connection rules below.

## 1. Table and starting chips

- Support 2–9 players at one table.
- Assign seats randomly and choose the first dealer button randomly.
- Every player starts with **5,000 chips**.
- Chips are whole numbers, with a minimum unit of **1**.
- Opening blinds are **25 / 50**, giving each player 100 opening big blinds.
- There are **no antes** and **no add-ons**.
- Close entry when the first hand is dealt. Returning to an existing live stack is permitted and is not a new entry.

## 2. Blinds and tournament clock

Each level lasts **10 minutes of unpaused tournament time**.

| Level | Small blind | Big blind | Starts after unpaused time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 25 | 50 | 0 minutes |
| 2 | 50 | 100 | 10 minutes |
| 3 | 75 | 150 | 20 minutes |
| 4 | 100 | 200 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | 150 | 300 | 40 minutes |
| 6 | 200 | 400 | 50 minutes |
| 7 | 300 | 600 | 60 minutes |
| 8 | 400 | 800 | 70 minutes |
| 9 | 600 | 1,200 | 80 minutes |
| 10 | 800 | 1,600 | 90 minutes |
| 11 | 1,000 | 2,000 | 100 minutes |
| 12 | 1,500 | 3,000 | 110 minutes |
| 13 | 2,000 | 4,000 | 120 minutes |
| 14 | 3,000 | 6,000 | 130 minutes |
| 15 | 4,000 | 8,000 | 140 minutes |
| 16 | 6,000 | 12,000 | 150 minutes |

After level 16, double both blinds at each further level: level 17 is 12,000 / 24,000, level 18 is 24,000 / 48,000, and so on.

The level clock keeps running during ordinary hands and thinking time. A hand retains the blinds and level it had when dealt; the next hand uses the level currently shown on the clock. Explicit pauses and rebuy decisions stop the level clock. Level times are not a promised total game duration.

## 3. Standard Hold’em play

Use normal 52-card Hold’em with no jokers. Deal two private cards per player; the shared board is dealt as flop, turn and river. Each player uses the best five-card combination available, using zero, one or two of their private cards. Use normal poker hand rankings, with suits equal. Action moves clockwise; betting before the flop starts after the big blind, and later rounds start after the button. [Basic Hold’em rules](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/texas-holdem/)

Use the **tournament dead-button method**, preserving blind obligations after eliminations. A vacant button position or a hand without a small blind is possible. When only two players remain, the button posts the small blind, acts first before the flop and last afterward. Adjust the transition to heads-up so a player does not receive consecutive big blinds. [Tournament button procedures](https://www.pokertda.com/view-poker-tda-rules/)

Hand categories, highest first: royal flush → other straight flush → four of a kind → full house → flush → straight → three of a kind → two pair → one pair → high card. Compare the relevant ranks and kickers within the best five cards only; a sixth or seventh card never breaks a tie. An ace can be high or low in a straight: A-2-3-4-5 is five-high, and sequences cannot wrap around. [Hand-ranking reference](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/rules/hand-rankings/)

## 4. Betting and all-ins

- Betting is **no-limit**, up to the chips a player has in play.
- A normal opening bet must be at least one current big blind. A full raise increases the wager by at least the last full bet or raise increment. A player may go all-in for less than that minimum.
- There is no cap on the number of raises.
- Raise size means the increase above the previous wager, not the final total.
- Example: a bet of 100 raised to 250 is an increase of 150; the next minimum raise is to 400.
- A short all-in does not automatically restore raising rights to players who have already acted. Cumulative short all-ins can restore those rights once they face a full qualifying increase.
- Example: A bets 100, B calls and C is all-in for 150. A and B may call 50 more or fold; they cannot raise unless later action reopens it.

Use standard full-raise and reopening rules throughout. [Poker TDA betting rules](https://www.pokertda.com/view-poker-tda-rules/)

## 5. Pots and tied hands

Only the chips in a player's stack at the beginning of a hand can be wagered during that hand. Return uncalled excess bets. Keep folded contributions in the pot and form main/side pots according to contributions and eligibility.

For example, if A contributes 100 all-in while B and C contribute 500 each, A, B and C contest a 300-chip main pot; B and C also contest an 800-chip side pot.

Evaluate and split each pot separately. Tied best five-card hands split their eligible pot equally. Distribute leftover single chips one each among tied winners, clockwise beginning after the button. Suits do not break ties. [Pot and all-in principles](https://www.pokerstars.com/poker/games/rules/)

## 6. Showdown and card visibility

At an ordinary showdown, automatically reveal **both hole cards of every player who has not folded**. There is no manual muck option at showdown.

When at least two hands remain and somebody is all-in, reveal all remaining hands as soon as **all betting is complete**, then finish the board. Do not reveal while other players can still bet into a side pot. All-in hands remain live and cannot be mucked. [All-in tabling rule](https://www.pokertda.com/view-poker-tda-rules/)

Always **run the board once**.

If everyone else folds, the remaining player wins without being required to show. Once a hand is over, a player may voluntarily reveal one or both of their own hole cards publicly. This permission never exposes another player's cards or allows early showing during a live hand.

**Rabbit hunting is disabled:** do not reveal undealt community cards after an uncontested hand ends.

## 7. One rebuy per player

A player who reaches zero chips after a hand may take **one rebuy in the entire tournament**, provided:

1. They have not already used their rebuy.
2. The hand in which they busted **began at level 1, 2 or 3**.
3. They accept the immediate between-hands offer within **30 seconds**.

A rebuy gives **5,000 fresh chips**, keeps the same seat and continues the same tournament entry. It is not an elimination and does not reset the player's rebuy allowance.

Eligibility is fixed from the hand's starting level. A level-three hand can finish after the clock reaches level four and still qualify. A hand begun at level four cannot qualify.

Offer the rebuy immediately after settling the hand. Pause play and the blind clock while decisions are pending. The rebuy's own 30-second countdown runs during this decision window, except during a separate host/manual/all-offline pause. If several players are eligible, offer their decisions together and wait until all are resolved.

Declining, timing out, already having used the rebuy, or busting in an ineligible hand eliminates the player. There is no later re-entry. Resolve all eligible rebuy decisions **before declaring a champion**.

## 8. Turn timer, absence and returning

Every normal decision has **30 seconds**. There is **no time bank**.

On timeout, **check if checking costs nothing; otherwise fold**. A player already all-in needs no further betting decision and stays eligible for the pots they cover.

An ordinary player's disconnection does not pause the game:

- Preserve their stack and seat, and continue posting their blinds.
- On their first missed decision, allow the remaining normal decision time before applying check/fold. Disconnecting does not restart the timer.
- While they remain absent, subsequent actions check/fold immediately.
- Voluntarily sitting out uses immediate check/fold, without an initial waiting period.
- A player may return while they have a surviving stack, or to an existing still-open rebuy decision. Returning does not create new chips, a new entry, a timer reset or a new rebuy allowance.

The host and all-offline exceptions in the next section take precedence over these ordinary absence rules.

## 9. Host and pauses

The **original host remains the host for the entire session**. There is no host transfer. The host role is separate from the dealer button.

If the original host leaves or disconnects, **pause the entire game immediately**, including a live hand, until they return. Preserve the exact hand state and remaining times. Freeze the blind clock, action countdown and any rebuy countdowns.

If every remaining tournament player disconnects, also pause and preserve the game. Resume only when the original host is present and at least one player still in the tournament is connected.

Only the original host may manually pause or resume, and only **between hands**. Manual pause is unavailable during a live hand; wait for that hand to finish. Host elimination alone does not remove the hosting role or end the session.

Track pause reasons separately: returning from a disconnection clears that automatic reason, but must not clear an independent manual pause or unresolved rebuy decision. Resume preserved timers rather than granting fresh time.

Any eligible cancellation can still be accepted while the game is paused.

## 10. Leaving versus ending the game

**Leave table / sit out** affects one player. Keep their stack, blinds and return rights under section 8, while the others continue. If that player is the original host, section 9 pauses the whole game until the host returns.

**End game / abandon game** cancels the whole tournament. **Any player still in the tournament** can do this; host permission and a vote are not required.

On an accepted cancellation:

- End immediately, even during a hand or pause.
- Mark the session **cancelled**, with **no tournament winner**.
- Void the current unfinished hand and restore all contributions from that hand, including blinds, to their owners.
- Keep the history and settled results of previously completed hands.
- Do not record this as a completed tournament or award a championship, league points or prizes.

A player awaiting an eligible rebuy decision is still in the tournament until that decision is declined or expires. A permanently eliminated player cannot cancel the game merely because they are still viewing it. The original host, if eliminated, retains pause controls but does not gain a cancellation permission beyond the chosen rule.

A tournament already completed or cancelled cannot be cancelled retrospectively.

## 11. Winner, finishing order and recorded results

After settling a hand and resolving eligible rebuys, the last player with chips wins.

Record the winner and finishing positions. Earlier eliminations place lower. When several players are eliminated in the same hand, compare their stacks **at the start of that hand**: a larger starting stack finishes higher, and equal starting stacks share a finishing position. Do not use the strength of their losing hands to break the tie. [Simultaneous elimination convention](https://www.pokerstars.com/help/articles/rules-table-master/213164/)

For tied positions, reserve the corresponding number of finishing slots: if two players tie for fourth, both display fourth and occupy places four and five. A successful rebuy does not create an elimination or duplicate finishing record.

There are **no league points**, **no prize-pool accounting**, and **no optional variants beyond those expressly listed above**. A cancelled session records its cancelled status and completed-hand history, rather than a tournament champion or completed finishing table.
